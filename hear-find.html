Hear & Find

A listening game for learning English words.

You hear an English word, then find that thing somewhere in a 3D house and tap it. No reading first and no multiple choice, just your ears and your eyes.

How it plays

Hear it. Find it. Tap it.

  1. 01

    Hear

    You hear an English word. Tap the speaker to hear it again as often as you like.

  2. 02

    Find

    Look for that thing somewhere in the 3D house. Drag to look around and walk from room to room.

  3. 03

    Tap

    Tap it. No reading first and no multiple choice, just your ears and your eyes.
