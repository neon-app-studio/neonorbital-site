AI and Tech

OpenAI countersues Apple, saying it took trade secrets (opens in a new tab)

The ChatGPT maker filed counterclaims Friday in California federal court, four months after Apple sued it over former employees.

OpenAI says Apple rehired a former employee who had spent six months at OpenAI and had seen plans for a new device and its future hardware roadmap. It also accuses Apple of pressing suppliers not to work on OpenAI devices.

Apple denied both claims. In a statement it called the counterclaims "a transparent attempt to distract from the substance of Apple's lawsuit."

Apple sued OpenAI and former employees Tang Tan and Chang Liu in July over alleged trade secret misappropriation, which they deny. OpenAI's filing says 400 employees have left Apple for OpenAI so far.

Nvidia tells partners to pause AI server shipments to four Southeast Asian countries (opens in a new tab)

Dell and Super Micro were told to hold servers bound for Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia, the Wall Street Journal reported.

The pause has no set end date while Nvidia checks whether regional partners meet U.S. export rules. It follows a Commerce Department rule this month aimed at Chinese companies using Nvidia chips remotely through data centers in those countries.

TechCrunch · Nov 26
This week

Nvidia's board reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO (opens in a new tab)

The board voted unanimously on Monday to bring him back as president, CEO and a director, effective immediately.

Nvidia said the board received input from senior levels of the U.S. government before deciding. A committee of independent directors is still investigating reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort.

Nvidia Newsroom · Nov 23

Canada adds C$700 million to its AI Compute Access Fund (opens in a new tab)

The top-up brings the fund to C$1 billion to help small and medium-sized companies pay for computing power.

The first new call for proposals covers health and life sciences. The government also announced more than C$52 million for 45 companies from the fund's first phase.

Government of Canada · Nov 26

New Zealand's governing coalition loses its majority in the final count (opens in a new tab)

National, ACT and NZ First now hold 65 seats, the same as the four parties on the other side.

Both blocs are one seat short in a 130-seat Parliament, which puts Opportunity leader Qiulae Wong and her six MPs in a pivotal spot. Recounts could still change the numbers.
