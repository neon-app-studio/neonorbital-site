Engineering project · Solana token

Sheen

Built by Neon Orbital LLC as an engineering project to test what it takes to design and launch a token end to end. Sheen is a fixed-supply SPL token on Solana: 1,618,000,000 SHEEN, a nod to the golden ratio. Mint authority is revoked, so no more can ever be made; there is no freeze authority; and 10% of the supply sits in a Bonfida vesting account on an on-chain, time-locked schedule that cannot be opened early. Sheen is not for sale.

  • 1,618,000,000Fixed supply
  • RevokedMint authority
  • 10% · BonfidaTime-locked vest
