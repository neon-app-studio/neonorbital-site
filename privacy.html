Privacy policy

Privacy, plainly

Last updated November 12, 2026. This policy covers the neonorbital.com website and the apps published by Neon Orbital LLC.

The short version

We do not collect, sell or share personal data. This website has no sign-up, no cookies, no ads and no analytics. Our apps have no accounts and keep what they save on your device.

This website

  • No cookies or tracking. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Fonts and images are served from this site.
  • Hosting. The company that hosts this site may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the service running and secure. We do not use these logs to identify you.
  • Music player. The player on the Music page loads only when you press play. Once loaded, the music service may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.
  • Links to other sites. Product, app, music and social links take you to other sites, such as Etsy and the App Store. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Shop orders

Our shop runs on Etsy. Etsy handles payments and downloads. We see the order details Etsy shares with sellers, such as your name and what you bought, and we use them only to deliver and support your order.

Hear & Find: Learn English

Hear & Find does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your stars, best scores, unlocked levels and settings are saved only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. The app never uses the microphone. The optional one-time unlock is sold and handled by Apple; we never see your payment details or Apple Account. The only network request is that purchase check, which goes through Apple.

Koi Cannon

Koi Cannon does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your stars, best scores and music setting are saved only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. The app does not send anything over the internet.

Children

We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing stored on our side. Deleting an app removes everything it saved on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If an app ever starts collecting data, this page and its App Store privacy label will say so first.

Contact
