Hear & Find

You hear an English word, then find that thing somewhere in a 3D house and tap it. No reading first and no multiple choice, just your ears and your eyes.

  • Over 250 everyday words and short phrases
  • Clear recorded voices, replay as often as you like
  • No account, no ads, works offline
  • Free to download, with an optional one-time unlock

Koi Cannon

A koi in a pot of water lobs water orbs at towers of jars, pots and boxes. Knock them all down before you run out of orbs. Everything runs on real physics, so no two crashes look the same.

  • 20 levels that keep getting taller and trickier
  • Tap to aim, or pull back and let go
  • Free, with no ads, no purchases and no account
  • Works without an internet connection
